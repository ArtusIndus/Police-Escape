# POLICE ESCAPE v 1.0

## Game Instructions

### OBJECTIVE

Escape from the police for as long as possible!

Drive through endless traffic, avoid other vehicles, and keep the police from catching you. The longer you survive, the higher your score and earnings will be.

### CONTROLS

**PC**

* **A / Left Arrow** — Steer left
* **D / Right Arrow** — Steer right
* **ESC** — Pause / Resume

**Mobile**

* Use the **left and right buttons** on the screen to steer your car.

### POLICE

The police will chase you during the game.

Keep your distance and avoid getting caught.

**Important:** The police only ends the game when the police car actually **touches your car**.

The police can also crash into traffic. If this happens, the police car disappears temporarily and will return after a short time.

### TRAFFIC

Other vehicles drive along the road. Avoid crashing into them.

If you hit another vehicle, you lose one life.

You start every game with **3 lives**.

When you lose all your lives, the game is over.

### SCORE

Your score increases automatically while you drive.

The longer you survive, the higher your score becomes.

Try to beat your personal high score!

### MONEY

You earn money based on the distance you drive.

Use your money to unlock new cars and roads.

### CARS

Visit the **Cars** menu to unlock and select different vehicles.

Each car has its own steering characteristics.

Some cars are faster and more responsive, while others are easier to control.

### ROADS

Visit the **Roads** menu to unlock different environments.

Available roads include:

* **City**
* **Highway**
* **Desert**
* **Night**
* **Snow**

Each road has its own visual style.

### TRAFFIC DENSITY

You can choose the traffic density in the main menu:

* **Low Traffic** — Fewer vehicles
* **Normal Traffic** — Standard difficulty
* **Heavy Traffic** — More vehicles and more danger

### PAUSE

Press the **Ⅱ button** or **ESC** on PC to pause the game.

While paused, you can:

* **Resume** — Continue playing
* **Quit** — Return to the main menu

### GAME OVER

The game ends when:

* You lose all **3 lives**, or
* The **police car touches your car**.

After Game Over, you can see:

* Your final score
* The distance you drove
* The money you earned

Then you can either play again or return to the main menu.

## THE CHALLENGE

How long can you escape?

**Drive. Dodge. Survive. Escape.**
